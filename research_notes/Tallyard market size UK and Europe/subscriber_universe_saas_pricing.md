# Subscriber universe and comparable SaaS pricing (UK and Europe)

Scope: how many organisations and professionals could pay Tallyard's subscription (supplier organisation £4,800 a year, buyer organisation £3,600 a year, professional seat £1,200 a year), and what comparable construction sustainability and reuse tools charge. Commission revenue is out of scope.

Method note for the report writer: every figure below comes from web search result extracts dated 2026-10-17. The page fetch tool failed for every host (DNS error), so no primary PDF or pricing page was opened in full, and the shared web search budget ran out before the final round (NFDC current membership, SpecifiedBy and Barbour Product Search pricing, LCA tool usage surveys were not searched). Figures quoted from search extracts should be checked against the linked primary source before publication. Where a figure comes from a commercial aggregator (IBISWorld, Reportlinker, directories) it is flagged.

## 1. Architecture: architects and practices in the UK and Europe

### Takeaway
The UK has about 40,900 registered architects (ARB, 1 October 2025) and roughly 3,600 to 4,000 RIBA Chartered Practices inside a wider pool of about 16,000 architecture businesses (IBISWorld estimate, 2024). Europe has about 580,000 architects across 29 or 30 countries (ACE Sector Study 2024), concentrated in Italy, Germany and Turkey; Germany alone has about 120,000 building architects (2024) and France about 29,500 (2023).

### Cited Findings
UK, architects (seats)
- ARB Register stood at 40,906 architects on 1 October 2025; this includes just under 1,000 reinstatements after non-payment of the 2025 retention fee (2025) ([ARB performance monitoring paper](https://arb.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/Performance-Monitoring-Cover-paper-1.pdf))
- ARB Register stood at 38,981 architects at the end of 2024 (2024) ([ARB 2025 year in review](https://arb.org.uk/publications/publications-list/annual-report/2025-year-in-review/); [Specification Online on ARB 2024 annual report](https://www.specificationonline.co.uk/articles/2025-07-11/arb-publishes-2024-annual-report-and-financial-statements))

### Inferences
- UK seat ceiling: 40,906 ARB registrants x £1,200 = about £49.1m a year (theoretical, includes overseas-based and non-practising registrants).
- UK practice ceiling: 3,650 to 4,000 RIBA Chartered Practices x £3,600 = about £13.1m to £14.4m a year; all 16,066 architecture businesses x £3,600 = about £57.8m (the latter is mostly micro firms unlikely to buy).
- If the RIBA size table is ordered smallest to largest (RIBA's usual presentation, not confirmed in the extract), the three largest bands hold 369 practices (about 10%) and the two largest 123: a plausible core for commercial and large projects. Flag as estimate.
- Early-adopter pool in the UK: roughly 1,100 Architects Declare company signatories (about 7% of all architecture businesses, or 25% to 30% of chartered practices), but only about 6% of chartered practices signed the RIBA 2030 Challenge by 2021.
- Europe seat ceiling: 580,000 architects x £1,200 = about £696m a year (theoretical). With about 46% owners or principals and about 70% solo practices, the count of practices is likely in the low hundreds of thousands, but the subset with 10 or more staff, which matters for an organisation licence, was not found.
- Germany: about 120,000 building architects and roughly one third self-employed suggests about 40,000 self-employed architects (estimate). France: 3,000 sociétés in Ile-de-France being about a third of the national total implies about 9,000 sociétés nationally (weak estimate from a secondary source).

### Gaps
- No current (2024 to 2026) count of RIBA Chartered Practices was found; the latest firm figures are about 3,600 (2019) and about 4,000 (Dezeen, 2021).
- No ONS count of UK architecture enterprises by size band after 2020, and no London count, was retrieved; London share of UK architects or practices was not found.
- ACE per-country architect and practice counts for Italy, Spain, Netherlands and others were not visible; the ACE PDF needs to be opened.
- No source gives the number of practices working on commercial or large projects.
- Eurostat enterprise counts for NACE M71.11 were not retrieved.

## 2. Developers, property owners and investors

### Takeaway
There is no single count of UK commercial developers. ONS gives 3,255 UK companies in SIC 41.10 with £2m to £20m turnover (undated FOI), within about 46,000 development businesses (IBISWorld, 2025 to 2026). Investor bodies give a tighter target list: INREV 516 members (145 in the UK, 2025), EPRA more than 280 members (2025), and 33 BBP Climate Commitment signatories with over £380bn AUM (2022).

### Cited Findings
- ONS FOI response: 3,255 companies in SIC 4110 (development of building projects) with turnover between £2m and £20m (date not stated) ([ONS FOI, turnover of companies](https://cy.ons.gov.uk/aboutus/transparencyandgovernance/freedomofinformationfoi/turnoverofcompanies))
- IBISWorld: about 45,946 building project development businesses in the UK (2026); 2025 estimates of 46,043 and 45,690 on different pages; the industry excludes construction work and project management (commercial estimate) ([IBISWorld UK building project development](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-kingdom/number-of-businesses/building-project-development/2400))
- British Property Federation members are owners, managers and developers of real estate plus supporting professional firms; no current total membership figure was found; BPF Futures (early-career network) has over 2,400 individual members (undated, recent) ([Wikipedia, BPF](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/British_Property_Federation); [Scottish Property Federation on BPF Futures](https://scottishpropertyfederation.org.uk/our-work/bpf-spotlight-series-bpf-futures))
- Better Buildings Partnership Climate Commitment: 33 property owner signatories covering over £380bn AUM worldwide (June 2022); launched September 2019 with more than 20 signatories; signatories must publish net zero pathways and delivery plans (2019, 2022) ([AREF on BBP](https://www.aref.org.uk/resource/the-better-buildings-partnership-bbp.html); [FashionUnited 2019](https://fashionunited.com/executive/report/leading-british-property-owners-sign-groundbreaking-carbon-commitment/2019092430094))
- INREV (European non-listed real estate investors): 516 members per 2025 AGM presentation, with 145 in the UK and 65 in Germany; 504 members in 2024 (2024, 2025) ([INREV AGM presentation 2025](https://www.inrev.org/system/files/2025-05/INREV%20AGM%20presentation.pdf); [Wikipedia, INREV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/INREV))
- EPRA (European listed real estate): more than 280 members including listed companies, investors and other participants (annual report published March 2026); more than 290 in 2023 lobbying register data (2023, 2026) ([EPRA annual report March 2026](https://www.epra.com/application/files/7217/7384/9229/EPRA_Annual_Report_March_2026_FV.pdf); [EPRA who we are](https://www.epra.com/about-us/who-we-are); [LobbyFacts, EPRA](https://lobbyfacts.eu/representative/4d07b4c346684729b5b0600e7dd5f899/european-public-real-estate-association))
- London Plan policy SI 7 requires a Circular Economy Statement, including a pre-demolition or pre-redevelopment audit, for referable applications; GLA guidance finalised 25 March 2022 (2022) ([GLA Circular Economy Statements LPG](https://www.london.gov.uk/sites/default/files/circular_economy_statements_lpg.pdf))
- GLA Circular Economy Monitoring 2023 (published November 2024) opens "There were 93 referable applications..." but the sentence was cut off in the extract, so what the 93 counts is unconfirmed (2023) ([GLA CE Monitoring 2023](https://www.london.gov.uk/media/107727/download)); the 2022 report covers statements submitted up to December 2022 and has a pre-demolition audits section (2022) ([GLA CE Monitoring 2022](https://www.london.gov.uk/sites/default/files/2024-06/Circular-Economy-Monitoring-2022-FINAL-June-2024.pdf))

### Inferences
- A realistic UK buyer or supplier organisation universe among developers and owners is in the low thousands: the 3,255 mid-size SIC 41.10 companies plus an unknown number above £20m turnover, against tens of thousands of micro developers. At £3,600 a year, 3,255 organisations is about £11.7m (estimate).
- A sharper London-first target list is the INREV UK membership (145), BBP signatories (33 in 2022, likely more now), EPRA's UK members, and the developers behind GLA referable schemes. If the GLA figure of 93 is annual referable applications reviewed, London generates on the order of 100 large schemes a year that need a pre-demolition audit (unconfirmed).
- Europe: INREV (516) plus EPRA (280+) gives under 800 large investor and listed property organisations, with overlap; this is the institutional owner core, not the full developer base.

### Gaps
- No current BPF member count, no count of active London commercial developers, and no BBP signatory count after 2022 were found.
- No count of owners of buildings due for demolition (the "donor building" owner) exists in the sources found; GLA monitoring PDFs should be opened for the pre-demolition audit counts.
- No European developer counts by country (Eurostat F41.10) were retrieved.

## 3. Demolition and deconstruction contractors

### Takeaway
The UK has roughly 790 to 815 demolition businesses (IBISWorld, 2024 to 2025) of which about 140 to 160 belong to the NFDC. Germany's Deutscher Abbruchverband has 863 members (2026), the Netherlands had about 590 demolition and asbestos firms with €1.65bn turnover (2018), and France has no reliable firm count in the sources found.

### Cited Findings
UK
- IBISWorld: 789 demolition businesses in the UK (2024, down 2.6%); 805 (2025, up 2.0%); another page states 815 for 2025; largest business Erith Contractors; moderate concentration (commercial estimates, inconsistent between pages) ([IBISWorld UK demolition, number of businesses](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-kingdom/number-of-businesses/demolition/2480); [IBISWorld UK demolition](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-kingdom/industry/demolition/2480/))
- IBISWorld UK demolition market size conflicts: £1.3bn (2026, SIC F43.110) on one page and £27.1bn (2026) on another; the second probably uses a much wider scope (conflicting, use with caution) ([IBISWorld UK demolition 2480](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-kingdom/industry/demolition-in-the-uk/2480/); [IBISWorld demolition 200552](https://www.ibisworld.com/united-kingdom/industry/demolition/200552/))
- NFDC: full corporate membership of 156 (2013 AGM); "more than 140 members" (undated wiki); members said to be involved in up to 80% of UK demolition works (self-reported claim) (2013, undated) ([KHL, All change at the NFDC](https://www.khl.com/news/all-change-at-the-nfdc/1083346.article); [Designing Buildings, NFDC](https://www.designingbuildings.co.uk/wiki/National%20Federation%20of%20Demolition%20Contractors))

- Germany: Deutscher Abbruchverband has 863 members (829 legal entities, 34 individuals) as of 1 August 2026; describes itself as more than 800 member firms in building and industrial demolition, rubble recycling and remediation, founded 1951, based in Cologne (2026) ([Bundestag lobby register R003926](https://www.lobbyregister.bundestag.de/suche/R003926))

- France: a commercial study page gives 23,523 firms (2021), €1.3bn turnover (2023) and 90,500 employees (2022) for "démolition-terrassement" (unsourced, includes earthworks); Businesscoot gives €32.6bn for the demolition works market (2021), a very different scope (conflicting) ([Propulse by CA](https://propulsebyca.fr/idees-business/demolition-terrassement/etude-marche); [Businesscoot](https://businesscoot.com/en/study/the-demolition-works-market-france))

- Netherlands: VERAS represents nearly 100 member firms (2024 EIB report); about 90 in 2015; a KvK-based commercial directory lists 2,819 "sloopbedrijven" including sole traders (2015, 2024) ([EIB marktstudie slopers 2024](https://www.eib.nl/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Rapportage-marktstudie-slopers-2024-definitief-pdf-.pdf); [CompanyData NL](https://companydata.com/nl/bedrijven/nederland/sloopbedrijven-nederland/))

### Inferences
- UK supplier organisation ceiling for demolition: about 800 firms x £4,800 = about £3.8m to £3.9m a year; the NFDC core (about 140 to 160 firms) x £4,800 = about £0.7m to £0.8m. The UK demolition segment alone is small; it matters for supply, not subscription revenue.
- Association membership is a good proxy for firms large enough to pay: UK about 150 (NFDC), Germany 829 firms (DA), Netherlands about 100 (VERAS). Sum about 1,080 firms across three countries.
- Raw enterprise counts for continental Europe (France about 22,000, Netherlands about 10,000 per aggregator) are inflated by site preparation and sole traders; the EIB count of 590 Dutch firms versus 10,000 in the aggregator shows the gap.

### Gaps
- No current (2024 to 2026) NFDC membership count; the last search on it was blocked by the search budget.
- No Eurostat F43.11 enterprise counts for EU27 plus EFTA; no total European demolition market turnover.
- No French firm count restricted to demolition (NAF 43.11Z) from INSEE Esane.
- EIB 2024 figures (2023 structure of the Dutch sector) not visible in extracts.

## 4. Surveyors and pre-demolition auditors

### Takeaway
RICS has about 114,000 qualified members worldwide and over 100,000 members in the UK (2024 to 2025), but no source counts firms that do pre-demolition or pre-refurbishment audits. In France, the PEMD diagnostic has been mandatory since 1 July 2023 with filing on a CSTB platform, but no count of PEMD diagnosticians or diagnostics was found.

### Cited Findings
- RICS: 142,032 members globally (2024, up from 140,209); UK membership passed 100,000 for the first time (2024) ([Property Industry Eye](https://propertyindustryeye.com/rics-membership-passes-100000-with-record-growth/))
- RICS annual review 2025: 142,521 members globally, of whom 113,977 qualified professionals and 28,544 trainees and candidates (2025) ([RICS annual review 2025](https://www.rics.org/about-rics/corporate-governance/annual-review-and-remuneration-report/rics-annual-review-2025))
- Material Index, a London platform, supports pre-demolition audits and Existing Materials Passports; its reports have been used on more than 70 UK projects (vendor-reported, undated) ([Stone Specialist profile](https://stonespecialist.com/news/profile-material-index); [Digital Catapult case study](https://www.digicatapult.org.uk/case-studies/study/material-index-uses-ai-to-boost-constructions-circular-economy/))
- France: PEMD regime in force from 1 July 2023; the client (maître d'ouvrage) must send the pre-works diagnostic and the post-works récolement form to the CSTB platform, the latter within 90 days of completion (2023, 2024) ([Banque des Territoires](https://www.banquedesterritoires.fr/dechets-du-batiment-un-arrete-precise-la-marche-suivre-pour-les-diagnostics-demolition-et); [Valobat note, June 2024](https://valobat.fr/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/Valobat-MOA-MOE-diagnostic-PEMD.pdf); [Ministere, diagnostic PEMD](https://www.ecologie.gouv.fr/diagnostic-produits-equipements-materiaux-et-dechets-pemd))
- France: PEMD diagnostic skills are recognised in national certifications RNCP 36312 and 38285 (current) ([France Competences RNCP 38285](https://www.francecompetences.fr/recherche/rncp/38285/); [France Competences RNCP 36312](https://www.francecompetences.fr/recherche/rncp/36312))
- London: referable applications must include a pre-demolition or pre-redevelopment audit within the Circular Economy Statement (2022 guidance) ([GLA Circular Economy Statements LPG](https://www.london.gov.uk/sites/default/files/circular_economy_statements_lpg.pdf))

### Inferences
- RICS membership is far too broad as a seat universe; only a fraction are building surveyors or work on pre-demolition audits. Even 2% of 100,000 UK members would be 2,000 seats (about £2.4m at £1,200), a purely illustrative figure.
- The audit market is better sized by demand (number of audited schemes, such as GLA referable schemes and French PEMD filings) than by supplier headcount, since audits are done by a mix of surveyors, demolition contractors, sustainability consultants and platforms such as Material Index.

### Gaps
- No count of UK firms offering pre-demolition audits (BRE SMARTWaste, ICE, RICS); no UK register exists in the sources found.
- No count of certified PEMD diagnosticians or annual PEMD filings in France; the PEMD scope threshold (believed to be demolition or major renovation over 1,000 m2 of floor area) was not verified in this session.
- RICS UK qualified member count (excluding trainees) not found.

## 8. Typical SaaS penetration in these segments

### Takeaway
