# Round 2 follow-up: client and consultant (r2f-client_consultant)

What the recording audit of 16 October 2026 found missing in the developer and consultant views, and what was done. One line per note, then every existing test assertion changed, with its reason (P8). No test was skipped, deleted or weakened.

## Notes

- **[36] Developer tracking (real gap, fixed).** Kilnworth Place now shows its BREEAM, LEED and WELL cells like any project ("Not targeted" in each, so the defaults that apply show), with "No consultant appointed yet" under the project name in the table and the cards. Each rating cell carries its state against target as a pill with icon and word (Merrowgate: BREEAM "Near target", WELL "On target"), from one new pure rule `goalState`/`goalReading` in `domain/engines/goals.ts` that the Sustainability tab's meters (`goalMeter`), the tracking (`TrackingScheme.state`) and the consultant home now share; `ratingState` moved there and is re-exported. The double full stop on the Sustainability tab is gone (`targetsNote` is one sentence with its own full stop). Decision: below 420 px the three scheme cells stack instead of squeezing three columns into a phone card.
- **[42] Consultant view (real gap, fixed).** The consultant's Overview opens on his own section (`ConsultantOverview`, test id `consultant-overview`): the four Sustainability figures against target, the statement card, the target meters, the certification snapshot and the requirements at risk, reusing the Sustainability tab's sections (exported) so both read alike; the shortlist and approval-count cards are left out for him, and the programme follows. The Projects list (cards and table) shows content by value and avoided carbon, secured, against the targets with state, the statement status and requirements ready and at risk, from the new `consultantProjectSummaries` selector. Decision: the programme stays visible under the consultant section rather than collapsed, to keep the programme package's card as it is.
- **[43] Overridable targets reaching the home (real gap, fixed).** `consultantDashboard` builds each project's meters from `goalRows`, the same rows as Goals and targets, so carbon is measured on secured materials (47.8, not 100.8) and an override (avoided carbon 80, content 10%) gives the same status as the tab. Meter labels come from `GOAL_DEFS` ("Reused and recycled content by value", "Upfront carbon avoided by reuse"); the tile reads "Avoided carbon, secured" with "{x} tCO2e more with approved materials" as a second line. Targets met now counts every goal with a target (2 of 12: upfront carbon and WELL met, BREEAM near), as the incidental note asked. The Overview's "tCO2e secured by approval" now reads "tCO2e approved".
- **[44] Circular economy statement (real gap, fixed).** `statementSections` rewritten: reclaimed materials grouped by status and family with no listing codes; certification one line per scheme (counts by status, credits at risk and with evidence ready named), without the statement's own measure; next steps derived from the records (reserve the approved materials, close each gap below target, record missing results, resolve credits at risk, set a target for a rating scheme recorded as a requirement but not targeted, then the step for the RIBA stage); carbon reads "No avoided carbon is on record yet" when there is none and names 100.8 as the approved addition and 148.6 as the total once reserved. List lines carry a bullet mark; `statementBlocks` turns them into `<ul>` on screen (`StatementText` in `ui/StatementSection.tsx`, also in the client's review dialog) and into real Word bullets (`numbering.xml` with its content type and relationship). Sending freezes the sections (`StatementDoc.sentSections`, seeded for Merrowgate): the client reads the sent version dated when it was sent; when the live text moves on the consultant sees "The figures have changed since you sent this on {date}" and "Send the updated statement" (which replaces the frozen copy and notifies "Updated circular economy statement ready for review"), and the client sees that what they read is the version sent on that date. A statement never sent reads "Draft" to the consultant and "Not sent yet" to the client and architect (`statementStatusText`), on the Sustainability card, the scheme card, the certification metrics and the tracking table.
- **[40] Reservations money (refuted, polish done).** The Reservations cost card has its own caveat, `RESERVATIONS_FINANCE_CAVEAT`, which says earlier deals are not in these totals and count in the secured carbon; Approvals keeps `FINANCE_CAVEAT`. Summary lines no longer mix pounds and pence: the cost card total and the Approvals decided rows use `savingWholeText` (whole pounds); package panels keep pence.
- **[C2] Consultant consent (real gap, fixed).** `Project.consultantLink` (optional; absent on seeded and older records counts as accepted). `consultantLinked(p)` in `domain/access.ts` requires the consultancy's acceptance and the client's, and `projectsFor` uses it, so a consultancy never sees a project before its client has accepted. Creating a project with a consultancy no longer notifies it; it is asked when the client accepts (`askConsultant` from `decideClientLink`), or at once when named later on an accepted project. `decideConsultantLink` (Accept or Decline with a note) notifies the architect and client, or the architect alone on a decline; the architect can ask again from Team. Project requests (`/app/projects/requests`) serves consultancies too, with a sidebar badge and the cards on the consultant home; the client's request card says which consultancy the architect proposes. Team, the setup checklist and the overview details say "waiting to accept", "asked once the client accepts" or "declined". Activity and notifications on reservations, decisions and sustainability reach only a linked consultancy (`linkedConsultantOrgId`).
- **[C3] New client (real gap, fixed).** "Client contact email" (optional) on New project and on Change client when a new client is named. It issues an invite tied to the project (`Invite.projectId`, `actions/clientInvite.ts`). The banner names the email with Copy invite link, Resend and Use another email, or offers the email field when none was given; bad, taken or unnecessary invites are refused with their own errors. Signing up with that email joins the client organisation (step 2 becomes "Join Harrowgate Homes", no organisation fields), as does opening the invite link; the practice is told "{name} joined {client}. They can now accept {project}", and the request waits on the new person's home. Accepting unlocks sending for approval.
- **Incidental: Isla on a pending project's address (fixed).** A person whose organisation has an unanswered request for that project gets "Accept {project} to open it" with a button to Project requests (`waitingRequestForPath`), for clients and consultancies; everyone else still gets the plain no-access page.

The live list (142.5) is labelled as such.
