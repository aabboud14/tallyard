Method note for the report writer. All findings below come from web search result summaries gathered on 17 October 2026. Full page reads (WebFetch) were blocked in this session (DNS failures on madaster.com, pappers.fr, northdata.com, shop.oneclicklca.com, backacia.com, nweurope.eu, europa.eu), and the shared web search budget ran out before Kamp C, Insert, Ombygning, Sorélo, Cyneo pricing and the Concular and Restado link could be checked. Treat each figure as "reported by the cited page" rather than read in full by me. Registry figures from aggregators (Proff, Lasso, Ownr, Pappers, Infonet, Duunitori, Finder) mirror filed accounts but were not checked against the official registry. Third-party estimates (Latka, PremierAlts, Tracxn, Dealroom) are flagged as estimates.

Currency conversions are mine, at assumed round rates for 2025 to 2026 that were not sourced in this session: EUR 1 = GBP 0.86; USD 1 = EUR 0.86; DKK 1 = EUR 0.134 (euro peg); NOK 1 = EUR 0.085; SEK 1 = EUR 0.09. The writer should replace these with sourced rates if precision matters.

## Key question 1: Who are the non-UK comparables, and what do they earn, raise, employ and handle?

### Takeaway
Every reuse pure play outside the UK that could be sized is small. Reported revenues sit between about EUR 0.7m (Cycle Up in 2020, Loopfront in 2024 to 2025) and EUR 1.6m (RotorDC), with Gamle Mursten at DKK 13.8m (about EUR 1.85m) of gross profit. Only New Horizon (about EUR 15m, demolition plus resale, undated) and One Click LCA (EUR 20.1m in 2024, carbon and LCA compliance software rather than reuse) are materially larger. Venture funding is also small: most players raised EUR 1m to EUR 5m, and only One Click LCA raised a large growth round (EUR 40m in 2023).

#### Company notes with sources

**RotorDC and Rotor (Belgium)**
- RotorDC reported 25 people employed (19.3 FTE) and annual turnover of EUR 1.6m, "rapidly growing", as of December 2022, in its New European Bauhaus prize application (self-reported, not checked against the NBB filing). [New European Bauhaus prizes](https://prizes.new-european-bauhaus.europa.eu/past-applications/rotor-deconstruction)
- RotorDC is a cooperative entirely owned by its employees, organised in incoming goods, processing and outgoing sales teams. [RotorDC about us](https://rotordc.com/about-us)
- Rotor (the non-profit design and research office) and RotorDC operate in parallel and employ about 35 people together. [rotordb.org, Rotor and RotorDC at Leonidas](https://www.rotordb.org/en/stories/rotor-and-rotordc-leonidas)
- In 2014 RotorDC sold almost only what its own workers dismantled; its shop now also sells materials from demolition contractors and real estate firms. Most materials spend under a few weeks in the warehouse because space is scarce. [rotordb.org](https://www.rotordb.org/en/stories/rotor-and-rotordc-leonidas); [RotorDC about us](https://rotordc.com/about-us)
- Rotor's dealer survey found many Belgian dealers still focus on rustic materials for the domestic and rural market, and most have been pushed out of cities because storage space in cities is expensive. [Material Times interview with RotorDC](https://www.materialtimes.com/articles/on-the-organization-of-the-material-environment-with-the-brussels-based-collective-rotor); [Rotor prestudy for a Brussels reuse centre](https://rotordb.org/en/projects/prestudy-development-reuse-center-social-economy-brussels) (the search summary did not say which of these two pages carried each point)

## Key question 2: What take rates or margins do reuse marketplaces and dealers actually earn?

### Takeaway
The only published marketplace commission found is Backacia's 30% default on the ex-VAT sale price, negotiable, which is nearly four times Tallyard's planned 8%. Contractor-backed models (Cyneo) charge annual membership fees instead of, or as well as, commission, and physical dealers keep the whole resale spread. Dealer gross margins on steel, bricks and floors were not found.

### Cited Findings
- Backacia charges a 30% commission on sales generated through the platform. [Backacia seller page](https://backacia.com/pages/vente-materiaux-btp-occasion-reemploi)
- Its terms say the commission is negotiated with the seller and, without negotiation, is fixed at 30% of the ex-VAT amount of each transaction. [Backacia CGV/CGU](https://backacia.com/pages/cgv-cgu)
- Backacia seller accounts are free or paid depending on the number of listings and projects. [Backacia seller page](https://backacia.com/pages/vente-materiaux-btp-occasion-reemploi)
- Cyneo (Bouygues Construction) charges community members an annual flat fee, which lets them sell through its marketplace and use shared storage and workshops. The fee level was not found. [L'Usine Nouvelle](https://www.usinenouvelle.com/article/cyneo-la-start-up-de-bouygues-construction-pour-developper-le-reemploi-des-materiaux-du-batiment.N2175852)
- RotorDC's turnover was EUR 1.6m on 19.3 FTE (December 2022), and its stock turns within weeks because of warehouse space limits. [New European Bauhaus prizes](https://prizes.new-european-bauhaus.europa.eu/past-applications/rotor-deconstruction); [RotorDC](https://rotordc.com/about-us)
- Gamle Mursten earned DKK 13.8m of gross profit in 2025 with 22 FTE; revenue is not disclosed. [Proff.dk](https://www.proff.dk/regnskab/gamle-mursten-aps/svendborg/t%C3%B8mmer-tr%C3%A6last-og-byggevarer-agentur-og-engros/GP7R4MI10LA); [Ownr](https://ownr.dk/companies/public-profile/10134412)
- Loopfront customers held materials with an estimated replacement value of EUR 16m and reported EUR 4m of savings from reuse (early 2023), against Loopfront revenue of about NOK 8m (about EUR 0.7m) a year. [Loopfront blog](https://blog.loopfront.com/blog/loopfront-customers-manage-material-with-a-combined-value-of-around-16-million); [Proff.no](https://www.proff.no/selskap/loopfront-as/trondheim/postordre-internetthandel/IF8MC0C10K9)
- Rotor found that reclamation dealers are pushed out of cities because urban storage is expensive. [Material Times](https://www.materialtimes.com/articles/on-the-organization-of-the-material-environment-with-the-brussels-based-collective-rotor)
- A Bouygues E&S release on a Savoie reuse platform said professionals were not yet committing to source from it. [Bouygues E&S](https://www.bouygues-es.fr/sites/france/files/2024-03/CP%20-%20Signature%20me%CC%81ce%CC%81nat%20BYES%20Enfin%20!%20Re%CC%81emploi%20Wiztrust.pdf)

### Inferences
- Backacia's 30% list rate suggests that small reclaimed lots need a high take rate to cover matchmaking, checks and logistics. Tallyard's 8% is defensible only on high-ticket lots (structural steel, large raised access floor or cladding batches), or if storage, testing and transport margins carry the economics, which is what Tallyard's revenue stack assumes. The report could present 8% as a low-end base case and 15% to 30% as the observed European range, with only one data point at the top (Backacia).
- Loopfront's revenue is about 4% to 5% of the EUR 16m of material its customers manage (NOK 8m is about EUR 0.7m). This is a rough proxy for what a software layer can capture relative to the material value it tracks. It mixes years and is indicative only.
- In practice, dealers and processors (RotorDC, Gamle Mursten, Mobius) capture the full spread between buying or dismantling cost and resale price, rather than a commission. They earn more per transaction, but tie up labour and warehouse space.
- Storage is a known bottleneck (Rotor's finding on dealers leaving cities), which supports storage brokerage as a London revenue line.

### Gaps
- No published dealer gross margins were found for reclaimed structural steel, bricks, raised access floors, stone cladding or glazing in continental Europe.
- Cycle Up, Restado, Concular and CCBuild marketplace commissions were not found.
- Cyneo's annual fee level and CCBuild's membership fees were not found.
- Interreg FCRBE and BAMB reports on price ratios (reclaimed against new) and dealer economics could not be read, because fetches were blocked.

## Key question 4: Which models have failed or pivoted, and why?

### Takeaway
The clearest failure is Cycle Up: France's best-backed reuse marketplace (Icade, Egis, Banque des Territoires) went into receivership in February 2026 after losing EUR 3.27m in 2023 on revenue last reported at about EUR 0.7m. Other pure marketplaces were folded into larger groups (Backacia into IXIO, Oogstkaart into New Horizon, possibly Restado into Concular). Software-led players (Loopfront, EME, Concular) have shifted towards passports and inventory tools, and still run small revenues with losses where filed.

### Inferences
- **Lesson 1: online-only marketplaces under-earn.** Cycle Up went into receivership despite blue-chip owners, Backacia needs a 30% list commission and sold to a group, and Oogstkaart was absorbed by a contractor. Matching alone does not cover the cost of checks, logistics and storage at reclaimed-material ticket sizes.
- **Lesson 2: physical processing with a guarantee works at small scale.** Gamle Mursten (bricks, profitable growth in gross profit), RotorDC (EUR 1.6m, cooperative) and Mobius (50,000 to 60,000 m2 a year of floor tiles) each industrialise one or a few product families and sell a warranted product. That fits Tallyard's focus on raised access floors, steel and cladding, but argues for partnering with processors rather than being a pure listing site.
- **Lesson 3: software that rides regulation scales; reuse-only software does not, yet.** One Click LCA (EUR 20.1m, plus 42%) against Loopfront (about EUR 0.7m, loss-making), Madaster (grant-funded, no revenue found) and EME (about USD 5m estimated). Tallyard's subscription line should tie itself to compliance outputs (pre-demolition audits, carbon reporting, planning conditions such as the London Plan circular economy statements) to command prices near the LCA benchmarks.
- **Lesson 4: contractors and public bodies are building their own infrastructure.** Cyneo (Bouygues), CCBuild (IVL, Skanska) and Opalis (Rotor, EU funded) occupy the directory and storage layers in their markets. Competition in London could come from contractor consortia as much as from start-ups.
- **Lesson 5: bundled revenue is the norm among survivors.** Survivors combine consulting or audits, deconstruction, reconditioning, storage and sales (RotorDC, New Horizon, Mobius, Cyneo). Tallyard's mix of subscriptions, commission, storage brokerage, testing referral and transport margin matches this pattern. The comparables suggest commission alone will be the smallest line at 8%.

### Gaps
- The causes of Cycle Up's insolvency (cash burn, demand, the workshop's capital needs) were not documented in press coverage that could be retrieved.
- No evidence was found on whether Madaster or Concular are profitable.
- Insert (Netherlands) and Kamp C (Belgium) histories were not researched, because the search budget ran out.
