# Demo script

Version 1.0, architect first. Two parts: the role journeys (about twelve minutes), then the twelve-step replay for anyone who wants every figure. Start from fresh data: press Reset demo data in the top bar. The demo date is fixed at 7 October 2026. The demo script panel in the top bar opens any step; "Set up to here" resets the data, replays the earlier steps and opens the screen.

Say early: this is a marketplace and a workflow tool, not an AI tool. Each role sees its own projects. The architect chooses on function, appearance and carbon; the asset owner approves, buys and decides what is visible. The architect never makes a deal.

## Part 1. The role journeys

### Architect (Priya Nair, Studio Oriel)

1. **Landing.** Pick Priya under "Start here". Point at the rail: Marketplace above, then Projects as folders, then New project. There is no Deals or Reuse plan entry.
2. **Browse.** Pictures lead: a public photo when the owner made one public, otherwise an illustration drawn from the survey record and labelled as such. Each card shows the typology and family, the sustainability band with the avoided carbon, quantity, availability, location and mass. Press the Structure chip, sort by most carbon avoided, and choose Merrowgate Wharf in "Checking against": every card gains a fit tag ("Available in time", "Tight", "Not available in time", "Available now").
3. **Listing L-9F4CQQ.** UB 457x191x67, 7.5 m: 48 pieces, 24.16 t, 41.0 tCO2e avoided, band High. The timeline strip shows today, the availability window (Q1 2027) and the date materials are needed on site (3 April 2028): "Available in time", about 13 months in storage. The guide price, £715 to £825 per tonne, is a secondary line. Download the 2D profile (DXF) and the 3D mesh (OBJ); BIM family is greyed as version 2. Nothing names the building, the owner or the date.
4. **Shared with you.** Lots an owner shared privately with one of Priya's projects, grouped by project. Accept the confidentiality terms for Merrowgate Wharf: five Tiverne House lots appear, tagged "Shared in confidence". Sallow Court and Ferrymoor Yard say no owner has shared lots with them yet.
5. **Wish list.** Save L-9F4CQQ to Merrowgate Wharf, then open the project's Wish list: pending, "Available in time" with the storage months, the band and the carbon, with totals by state. The header line says approval and purchase sit with the client. Open Spec sheet: the draft from pending items, public fields only, with the caveats; "Print or save as PDF" and "Download spreadsheet". Back on the wish list, press "Send 1 pending to the client".
6. **Match schedule** in the project folder is greyed with "Version 2. Not in this release.": matching a BIM model or a steel schedule against the whole marketplace is where this goes next.
7. **Saved** holds the pitch pine joists: "nice, but no project yet". Move to a project later. **New project** takes a name, a client, a type, a region and the date materials are needed on site.

### Client (Isla Brennan, Lantern Quay Developments)

8. **Approvals.** The items Priya sent, with the visual, the specification summary, quantity, availability, timeline fit, carbon and guide price. Approve L-9F4CQQ with a short note.
9. Switch back to Priya: the row reads Approved with the date and Isla's note, and it can no longer be removed. The spec sheet's Approved tab lists it.
10. Isla's Match schedule (advanced), Reuse plan and Deals run the existing steps 5 to 9 below: the package, the negotiation agent, her approval, the seller's approval and the delivery booking.

### Surveyor and selling owner

11. **Dana Kowalski, Tarnbrook Deconstruction**, at phone width: Clients, Ostlea Estates, Tiverne House, Capture. Type the step 1 description, press Assist, add a photo, choose condition A. Expected availability defaults to the building's dismantling start (January 2027); leave it so the step values hold. Save: TH-12 appears in the inventory with its expected month. Then Brackwater Estates, Harrowden Court: three private items expected August 2027, none from Tiverne House.
12. **Tom Ashby, Ostlea Estates**: Tiverne House only. Priority shows the route on the five-step UK decision tree with its legend. Listings and visibility offers Private, Shared privately with selected projects, and Published to the marketplace; the sharing list shows each project blind (organisation type, project type, region, the quarter it needs materials). Harrowden Court is nowhere in his rail and not reachable by URL.

### Consultant and operator

13. **Marcus Lindqvist, Halewick Sustainability**: Merrowgate Wharf, Wish list review (read only, items by state with mass and avoided carbon), then Compliance (step 10) and Durnley House waste and reuse (step 11).
14. **Platform operator**: Ledger and Model comparison (step 12).
