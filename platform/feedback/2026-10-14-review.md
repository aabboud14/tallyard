# Review of 14 October 2026: improving the dashboards and flows

Source: notes of a walkthrough of the Tallyard platform across all roles with the partner (Junio Valerio D'Angelantonio), 14 October 2026. Judged a good starting point; asks for richer graphics, RIBA-stage timelines, financial and sustainability metrics, and location-based compliance tracking. Kept as written; the decisions taken on each point are in `BRIEF.md` section 14.

## Overall look and architect dashboard

- Graphics are a good start but look a bit AI-ish; OneClickLCA's site is the benchmark to beat.
  - Homepage structure (who is this for, how it works) works well.
- Dashboards across all roles feel blank and text-heavy; need colour-coded metrics and project thumbnails.
- Move the "new for your project" feed into Discover; rename Discover (for example "Marketplace") and "waiting on client" to "waiting for approval".
- Left nav projects should be filterable alphabetically or by client.

## Marketplace and material pages

- Discover should open on category folders (structure, envelope, finishes) with thumbnails, then allow scrolling all.
- "Checking against project" is useful but needs more visibility; default should be "No project".
- Material page: simpler background, spinnable 3D view, and a Revit-compatible format beyond OBJ.
  - The downloaded 3D model was surprisingly good.
- Replace Q1, Q2, Q3 availability with approximate dates or a date slider; drop guide price and seller details for architects.
  - Priority order: images and 3D, save to project, availability, sustainability, specs.

## Project setup and timeline

- Each project needs a timeline of design stages (likely RIBA), site start and practical completion.
  - Material availability should overlap it, showing today's stage; the programme should dominate the overview.
- Region and local authority dropdowns should pull relevant legislation and standards to select, optional for architects.
- Project type options need expanding plus manual input; recent activity should be collapsible.
- New projects should visually highlight next steps; draggable availability bars kept as a possible future feature.

## Surveyor and asset owner flows

- Surveyors need a standalone Capture option and the ability to create projects before a client invite arrives.
  - Option to notify a testing company and invite the deconstruction company to set availability dates.
- Client linking by architects should require approval, otherwise any architect can add them.
- Asset owners may both donate and build; buildings must be flagged as deconstruction or new project.
  - Rename "asset owner" to "donor building" for demo clarity; "Priorities" needs a new name.
- Add link-based sharing with revocable access; building structure input should be granular (frame, slabs).

## Developer and sustainability consultant views

- Developer dashboard needs cost, savings, carbon, % reuse, and BREEAM, LEED, WELL and circular economy tracking.
  - Parallel timeline across projects plus a to-do list of approvals and reviews.
- Approvals and reservations need financial metrics; reservations need a carbon summary.
- Across the software, track planning and sustainability requirements per borough and region (for example GLA, net zero carbon standard).
- Consultant view should differ from others: add a goals and targets tab with overridable targets, an auto-written circular economy statement.
  - Carbon tab becomes a wider sustainability tab; certification mapping should be interactive.
