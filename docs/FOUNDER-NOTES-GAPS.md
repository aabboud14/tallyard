# Founder's notes against the prototype

The founder's process description arrived after the build. This compares it with Tier 1.

## Covered, working end to end

- Developer 1 surveys a building and lists materials; developer 2's architect finds them at RIBA Stage 2 with real dimensions and matches them to a steel schedule.
- Storage at partner facilities, testing, transport and the timing-gap costs, priced against buying new.
- Consultant outputs: reused content by value, avoided carbon, waste diversion, workbook exports for the Circular Economy Statement, BREEAM and LEED mapping.
- Revenue: subscriptions for active and passive users, commission, storage brokerage, testing referral; agency against principal comparison.
- Property managers, waste management firms, manufacturers with surplus and contractors appear as sellers of seeded stock.
- Phone capture with photos; spreadsheet import and export are real.

## Covered as a labelled stand-in or stub

- Each AI step (categorise, rank, dynamic pricing, carbon estimates, negotiating agent, transport agent) runs as a fixed rule with the L2 or L3 label.
- BIM import, photo recognition and PDF bill reading are the three stubs.
- On-site testing and sampling are a fee line and a referral, not a service flow.

## Deliberately left out (brief/01-BRIEF.md section 3)

- Rent premium and ESG-driven rent uplift (unverified figures).
- Vacancy and pipeline data as a product (conflicts with private pipelines); Tier 2 adds aggregated demand insights with a five-organisation threshold.
- Marketing narrative, the 3D model, post-approval document uploads.
