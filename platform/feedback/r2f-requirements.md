# Round 2 follow-up: location-based requirements across the software

From the recording audit of 16 October 2026 (package `requirements`). One line per note, then every existing test assertion changed and why.

## Notes

- **[20] Region and local authority pull the requirements (REAL GAP).** Fixed all three parts. (1) A project created with the requirements panel closed now reads the same everywhere: the overview card says "10 suggested for Camden, Central London", explains that the suggestions are tracked until the architect chooses, and offers Review requirements; the Requirements tab, the requirements dialog (which starts from the tracked set) and the setup checklist ("10 suggested requirements are tracked until you review them", action Review requirements) all agree with `applicableRequirements`. Decision: kept "closed means not yet reviewed" rather than saving the suggestions as a choice, so the checklist step stays honestly to do; the form now says "Left closed, the suggested ones are tracked until you choose." The form also submits the ticks whenever the picker was opened or changed (a change followed by closing the panel was lost before), and an explicit empty choice ("Clear") is kept as chosen instead of falling back to the suggestions (`projectRecord` takes `requirementsReviewed`). (2) Changing region or borough after touching the picker now applies the `requirementsAfterMove` rule: ticks that still apply stay, the new place's suggestions are ticked, and a status line says so ("1 more is suggested there and now ticked."). (3) Generated local entries (and the new Wales and Scotland national entries) carry `placeholder: true` and show a "Not yet summarised" pill in the picker, the overview card and the Requirements tab. Decision: did not hand-write policies for Newham, Ealing, Tower Hamlets or Wandsworth, because I could not verify their current policy names and an invented summary would be worse than a labelled placeholder. Borough finding (fix c): the local authority dropdown now lists every authority grouped by region (this region first, native optgroups so typing finds a name), and choosing one sets the region; the region dropdown names the boroughs a London region covers.
- **[41] Track requirements per borough and region across the software (REAL GAP).** Consultant home cards now show a Requirements line ("0 of 10 ready", an at-risk pill), each at-risk item as a link straight to it on the Requirements tab (Merrowgate's BREEAM item), the borough and region under the client name, and Requirements joins the card links. The client tracking table names "For Newham, Inner London East" in the requirements cell (kept in that cell, not under the project name, to stay clear of the client_consultant package's edits to the name cell). The overview card link reads View requirements for the architect and Track requirements for the client and consultant (`canTrack`). Not done: a requirements-at-risk metric tile on the consultant home (optional in the brief; the tiles belong to other packages).
- **[C7] The region list (REAL GAP).** (a) London regions now follow the ONS split of Inner and Outer London: Brent and Merton moved to Outer London West, Greenwich to Outer London East; Central London stays as the seven inner authorities around the centre. Decision: ONS rather than the 1963 statutory list, because the two differ only on Haringey, Newham and Greenwich, ONS is the definition current statistics use, and it keeps the seeded Merrowgate Wharf (Newham) and every other seeded project and building in its region, so no transport figure moved. The borough-first dropdown covers "one searchable list that sets the region". (b) Added East Midlands, West Midlands, North West, North East, Yorkshire and the Humber, South West, Wales and Scotland, each with ten authorities and "Another local authority". In Wales and Scotland the national group shows a "not yet summarised" placeholder plus the UK-wide standards, never NPPF or Part L. Hub distances for the new regions are Tallyard estimates, and their parameter rows give that as the source (not the 7 October call). The region dropdown is grouped London, England outside London, Wales and Scotland.
- **[C1] Requirements on donor buildings (REAL GAP).** New donor dataset in `reference/requirements.ts` (`appliesTo: 'donor'`): a national demolition notice placeholder (Section 80; Wales and Scotland variants), the London Plan pre-demolition audit and diversion target, and the written City of London Carbon Options, Westminster retrofit first and Camden CC1 entries reused as they are; other authorities get a labelled placeholder. A Requirements tab on every donor building for the owner and the surveyor (both set status and keep evidence; a contractor alone and buyers cannot open it), a "Requirements 1 of 4 ready, 1 at risk" line on the owner home building card and the surveyor building row, linking to the tab. When the surveyor marks one at risk or evidence ready the owner is notified. Tiverne House is seeded (Carbon Options evidence ready, pre-demolition audit in progress, diversion at risk). Stored as `AppData.buildingTracks`, read defensively so a sandbox stored before today still opens. Help, Methodology lists the new summarised entries.
