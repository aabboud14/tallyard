# r2f-market: Navigation, Marketplace and material page

Follow-up to the recording audit of 16 October 2026 (the partner's notes of 14 October). One line per note, then the incidental issues, then every existing test assertion changed and why.

## Notes

- [4] Fixed. The home's "{n} new materials fit" link now goes to `/app/marketplace?project={id}#new-for-projects` (`newForProjectHref` in `store/paths.ts`, new field `newFitsHref` on the architect home card). The row has the id `new-for-projects`; Discover scrolls to it on arrival, with a scroll margin measured from the sticky top bar and toolbar at the moment of scrolling (no fixed guess), and leaves Back alone. The order stays folders first. Decision: the jump is a chip "{n} new for {project}" beside the fit chips in the checking strip and, with no project checked, a "{n} new for your projects" chip beside "Browse by category", so the feed is reachable from the top in both states. On a phone the chip keeps its full words (no "10 new" alone, which read as a fit count) inside a 44 px hit area. Text comes from the pure `newForProjectsJump`. Checked at 1440, 1024 and 390 px: the heading clears the sticky bars and the first tiles are in view.
- [6] Fixed. The link-status pill on the projects list card and the project header reads "Client to accept", neutral tone, MailQuestion icon, with a title explaining it, so it no longer looks like the shortlist's "Waiting for approval". The overview banner sentence is unchanged. The optional SetupChecklist icon was left to the programme package, which owns that file.
- [7] Fixed. The sidebar projects menu is a labelled control naming the current order ("A to Z", "By client", "Recent") with ListFilter and ChevronDown icons, ink-soft text (about 7:1), 44 px tall below lg. The state dot is gone; the client chip is unchanged. Test id and aria-label kept. No find box, collapse all or sorting on /app/projects. There is no `docs/screens` folder in the platform, so no shots to refresh; the throwaway shots were reviewed instead.
- [C4] Fixed. A project in the address (`?project=`) applies to that visit only: `useCheckingProject` no longer stores it. Only a choice made in the checking strip or on the material page (Choose, Change, Stop checking) is remembered. The sidebar Marketplace therefore opens on No project after following a home link, and the folders no longer show a project's counts on later visits. Recorded in BRIEF 14.1. Caveat: a sandbox that already holds a silently stored choice from the old behaviour keeps it until the person clears it with the X in the strip; stored state is the upgrade package's area.
- [15] Fixed. `specificationView` gives the architect a schedule without the Price section (guide price, market signal) and without the market signal caveat, through the new pure `specSheetWithoutPrice`. The screen, the print view and the spreadsheet all read the view, so all three lose it. The client's schedule keeps both. The engine output and its tests are unchanged. Recorded in BRIEF 14.1.
- [10] Fixed. The material page stage draws the technical drawings (steel, curtain wall, raised floor) on plain white with no drafting grid, through a new `plain` option on `SectionAxo`, `PanelElevation`, `PanelAxo`, `MaterialArt`, `MaterialIllustration` and `MaterialImage`. Cards, thumbnails, the sign-in panel and the gallery keep the paper board. Texture swatches (timber, brick, stone, precast) are pictures of the material and stay.
- [14] Partly mine, done. Methodology now reads "Timing three-month window 5 or month 15" and the open question "Are region and a three-month window coarse enough". The dead quarter code was relabelled rather than deleted, because deleting it would delete existing tests (P8): `programmeTimeline` ticks read "October 2026", `nextQuarterStarts` options read "By 1 January 2027", and `format.quarter()` is marked deprecated (not for display) and kept for its test. `formatQuarter` stays (programme.ts and `PublicListing.availability.label` use it; that label is never rendered). Decision on cards and share-link cards: they keep the short form "Expected Jan to Mar 2027", because the round 2 design (marketplace-material.md, decisions) specifies the short form on cards; the material page, specification and exports use the full form. The programme axis itself belongs to the programme package.

## Incidental issues

- Sidebar folders kept open: fixed. A folder opened because the page is inside it (or its link was followed) closes again when the person moves on; one the person opens or closes by its chevron stays as they left it (`app/sidebar/expansion.ts`, pure and tested; older stored lists still read).
- "In time for 3 of your projects" and the same "10 new materials fit" on every card: not changed. It is the seed (every recent listing fits all three projects); the code counts per project. Following the home link now opens the row checked against that project, whose captions name it.
- Envelope chip highlighted at 390 px: not a bug. It is the hover style under the mouse left by the audit script; with the mouse moved away every unselected chip has no background.
- UC column drawn lying down while the IFC stands it up: fixed. `meshFor` stands UC sections upright (length up Y), as `IfcColumn` places them; UB beams still lie along X.
- Touch scroll over the 3D viewer tilting the model: fixed. On pointercancel the viewer goes back to the angles it had before the gesture instead of keeping the partial drag.
- 3D caption telling phone users to use the arrow keys: fixed. On a coarse pointer it says "Drag sideways to turn it."
- Card availability abbreviated against BRIEF 14.1: kept, see [14].
- Command palette cannot find building tabs: fixed. Tabs inside project and building folders are indexed as pages with the folder's name as the sublabel ("Recovery plan, Wexcombe House"); they are found by a search and left out of the list before one.
- Share link cards abbreviate availability: kept, see [14].
- Material page "{project} start": fixed. The timeline marker reads "Materials needed from {date}", as the fit text does; the availability slider's project marker reads "Materials needed for {project}".
- Following a home link changes the folders on later visits: fixed by [C4].
